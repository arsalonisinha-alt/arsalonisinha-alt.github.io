01 / About

Saloni Sinha · Edinburgh

I'm an architect and interior designer drawn to the point where a considered plan becomes a space people genuinely want to be in.

Over 2.5 years of professional practice I've worked across residential, commercial, and institutional projects, from concept development and space planning to material selection, construction documentation, and site coordination. I'm as comfortable resolving a detail in Revit as I am building a material palette or producing a polished render for clients.

I'm now completing my MA in Interiors, Architectural & Spatial Design at Edinburgh College of Art, University of Edinburgh, exploring adaptive reuse and interior narratives, the ideas running through the projects below.
